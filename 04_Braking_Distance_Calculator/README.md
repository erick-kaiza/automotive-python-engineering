------OVERVIEW-----
This Python Project calculates a vehicle's reaction distance,braking distance,and total stopping distance based on vehicle speed,tyre-road coefficient of friction and driver reaction time.

The project demonstrates application of Newtonian mechanics and the Work-Energy Principle to vehicle braking analysis.

-----ENGINEERING CONCEPTS-----
    -Kinetic Energy
    -Work-Energy Principle
    -Friction Force
    -Driver reaction
    -Vehicle Safety
    -Stpping Distance Analysis

-----ENGINEERING EQUATIONS-----
#Speed conversion
speed(m/s)=speed(km/h)/3.6
#Reaction Distance
Reaction Distance= speed x Reaction time
#Braking Distance
braking distance=speed **2/(2 x coefficient of friction x gravitational acceleration(g))
#Total Stopping Distance
total stopping distance=reaction distance + braking distance

-----ENGINEERING NOTES-----
This Calculator assumes:
    -Level road sufrace
    -Constant coefficient of friction
    -Maximum braking effort
    -No Aerodynamic drag
    -No brake fade

Real Vehicles are also affected by tyre characteristics,brake temperature,suspension behaviour,weight transfer,aerodynamic downforce and road conditions.
