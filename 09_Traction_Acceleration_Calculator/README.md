This Python project models the traction-limited acceleration of a vehicle while accounting for aerodynamic downforce.
The project builds on previous vehicle dynamics concepts introduced in earlier projects and introduces a simple time-stepped simulation.
Instead of assuming a constant amount of aerodynamic downforce,the program calculates downforce dynamically as vehicle speed increases.
However,it should be noted that the tractive force at the tyres and hence the acceleration times are coming from a purely simple model,the calculator does not account for various real-world factors that affect vehicle aerodynamica that include(but not limited to):
    -rolling resistance
    -aerodynamic drag
    -tire degradation
    -change in road conditions
    -braking and acceleration forces 
    -etc
