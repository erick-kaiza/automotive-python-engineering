This Python Project calculates the aerodynamic drag force acting on a vehicle and determines the power required to overcome this drag at a certain speed and determine the theoretical top speed of the vehicle.

This calculator demonstrates the relationship between : 
    -engine power
    -vehicle speed
    -aerodynamic drag
    -power requirement
Aerodynamic drag increases with the square of velocity,while the power required to overcome it increases approximately with the cube of velocity.
This is why aerodynamic efficiency becomes increasingly important at high vehicle speeds.

However,this is a simplified model that does not account for various real world factors that affect aerodynamics in vehicles such as rolling resistance,drivetrain losses,downforce,wind,tyre effects,road gradient etc.
Therefore,the calculated top speed represents a theoretical aerodynamic limitv rather than an exact real world vehicle top speed.
